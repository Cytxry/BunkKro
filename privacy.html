Privacy Policy

1. Who runs BunkKro

BunkKro is a personal project run by Charan, an individual based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. It is not operated by a company. In this policy, “we” means Charan as the operator of BunkKro.

2. Information we collect

When you create an account

  • Your email address and a password. The password is stored only in a protected (hashed) form; we cannot read it.
  • If you use “Continue with Google”: your name, email address and profile photo from Google (see section 4).

What you put into BunkKro

  • Your subjects, attendance numbers and the classes you mark as attended, missed or cancelled.
  • Your timetable, holidays, extra classes and days that follow another day's timetable.
  • Your settings: attendance target, how your college rounds percentages, semester dates, theme, and a display name if you set one.

When you import a timetable

  • A record of the import: when it happened, how many classes were read, how many you corrected, and when you confirmed it. The photo or PDF itself is not uploaded (see section 5).
  • Only if you turn on “Help the AI learn from my fixes”: the corrections you made to a timetable BunkKro read. This never includes your attendance. Turning the option off deletes what was shared.

Other

  • If you tap “Tell me when Pro launches”, we record that and the date on your account, so we can tell you once when a paid plan is available. No payment information is collected; BunkKro has no payments.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not collect your location, contacts or device identifiers.

3. How we use it

We use your information only to provide BunkKro to you:

  • to create your account and log you in;
  • to calculate your attendance and how many classes you can miss;
  • to keep your data in sync across your devices and the browser extension;
  • to send account emails, such as password resets and confirmation codes;
  • if you have chosen to share timetable corrections, to improve how BunkKro reads timetables.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone for their own purposes. By creating an account you agree to this use. You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your account.

4. Google sign-in

If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with BunkKro. We ask for nothing else from your Google account: not your contacts, files, calendar or email.

We use this information only to create your account, log you in, and show your name and photo inside BunkKro. We do not transfer it to others except the service providers listed in section 7 that run BunkKro for us, and we do not use it for advertising. Our use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy. You can remove BunkKro's access at any time from your Google Account's security settings.

5. Timetable photos and PDFs

When you import a timetable from a photo or PDF, the file is read by a scanner that runs inside your own browser. The file is not uploaded to BunkKro. Only the timetable you check and confirm is saved to your account.

To work, the scanner downloads its program and text-recognition files from public file-delivery services, the same way a website loads its code. Those services see your IP address, but not your timetable.

We plan to add a more accurate timetable reader that runs on a server. That would mean sending the file to that server. It is not in use today. Before it is, we will update this policy to say what is sent, where it is processed, who can access it and when it is deleted, and we will tell you in BunkKro before your first upload.

6. What stays on your device

Your browser stores your login session, a copy of your data so BunkKro opens quickly, and preferences such as the theme. These are needed for BunkKro to work, so we do not ask for consent to them and there are no optional cookies to switch off. The browser extension reads your login from the BunkKro website so you can mark classes from it; it keeps that login in your browser and does not read other websites.

7. Who we share it with

We share your information only with the services that run BunkKro for us, and only so they can provide that service:

  • Supabase stores your account and data, handles logins, and sends account emails.
  • Vercel hosts the website. Like any web host, it processes your IP address when you visit.
  • Google, only if you use Google sign-in.

We may disclose information if Indian law requires it, for example in response to a valid order from a court or government authority.

8. Where it is stored

Your account and data are stored on servers in Australia. By using BunkKro you agree to this transfer outside India, which Indian law permits. The website itself is delivered from servers in several countries.

9. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. When you delete your data or your account, it is removed from our active database straight away. Copies may remain in our providers' routine backups for a limited time until those backups are replaced.

10. Your rights

  • Access: Settings → Your data → Download my data gives you a copy of everything stored in your account.
  • Correction: you can edit your information in BunkKro at any time, or ask us to correct it.
  • Deletion: you can delete your data and your account yourself (see section 11), or ask us to.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off timetable sharing in Settings, or delete your account.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to: write to us.
  • Raise a grievance with us (see section 15). If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

11. Deleting your account

In Settings → Your data, choose Delete my data and type DELETE to confirm. This deletes your subjects, attendance, timetable, holidays and timetable imports on every device. You are then offered to delete your login as well, which removes your account completely. This cannot be undone. If you prefer, email us and we will do it for you.

12. Security

Information travels over encrypted connections, and our database is set up so each account can reach only its own data. We take reasonable care to protect your information, but no online service can be completely secure. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you and the authorities as Indian law requires.

13. Age

BunkKro is intended for people aged 18 and over. If you are under 18, please do not use BunkKro. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how your information is used, we will tell you in BunkKro before it takes effect.
