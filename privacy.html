BunkKro helps you track college attendance and work out which classes you can miss. This policy explains what we store, why, who helps us run BunkKro, and what you can do about it. We wrote it to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules.

Who we are

BunkKro is run by [your name or company], [city], India. For anything about your data, write to [contact email]. That address is also our grievance contact; we reply within 7 days and resolve complaints within 30 days.

What we store

  • Your login. Your email address and password (kept only as a secure hash by our login provider). If you log in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google.
  • What you put in. Your subjects, attendance numbers and marks, timetable, holidays, semester dates, and settings such as your attendance target.
  • Timetable imports. When you import a timetable, we keep a record of the import: how many classes were read, how many you fixed, and when you confirmed it. The timetable photo or PDF itself is not uploaded to us: the built-in scanner reads it inside your browser, and BunkKro AI reads it on your own computer.
  • Fixes you choose to share. Only if you turn on “Help the AI learn from my fixes”, we keep the corrections you made to a read timetable, to improve the reader. Never your attendance. Turning it off deletes them.
  • On your device. Your browser keeps a copy of your data and your preferences (like the theme) so BunkKro opens fast and works offline. We use no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics.

Why we use it

Only to run BunkKro for you: to log you in, keep your data in sync across your devices, and do the attendance maths. We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone for their own use. By creating an account you agree to this use; you can withdraw at any time by deleting your data (see below).

Who helps us run BunkKro

  • Supabase: our database and login service, where your account and data are stored.
  • Vercel: hosts the website.
  • Google: only if you choose “Continue with Google”, to log you in. The site's font is also loaded from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address.
  • jsDelivr: delivers some of the website's code libraries.
  • When Pro launches, a payment provider will handle payments. We will never see or store your card or UPI details.

These services may store data outside India. They process it only to provide their service to us.

The browser extension

The BunkKro extension reads your login from the BunkKro website so you can mark attendance from your browser. It keeps that login in your browser and talks only to BunkKro's database. It does not read other websites.

How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. When you delete your data or your account in Settings, it is deleted from our database straight away. Copies in our providers' backups are removed as those backups expire, within [30] days.

Your rights

  • See your data: Settings → Your data → Download my data gives you everything in one file.
  • Correct it: edit anything in the app, or write to us.
  • Delete it: Settings → Your data → Delete my data. You can then delete your login too.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off sharing in Settings, or delete your data.
  • Nominate someone to act for you if you die or can't act yourself: write to us.
  • Complain: write to [contact email] first. If you're not satisfied, you can go to the Data Protection Board of India.

Keeping it safe

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Every row in our database is locked to its owner, so no other account can read it. If a breach ever affects your data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

Age

BunkKro is meant for college students aged 18 or over. If you're under 18, use it only with your parent's or guardian's consent.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
