BunkKro

An attendance tracker for college students. Mark your classes as they happen, and BunkKro works out your attendance and how many classes you can still miss without falling below your college's minimum.

Open BunkKro

Free · no ads · we don't sell your data

What it does

  • Shows your attendance for each subject and overall, rounded the way your college rounds it.
  • Tells you how many classes you can miss, or how many you need to attend to get back to your target.
  • Reads your timetable from a photo or PDF, or lets you set it up by hand.
  • Handles holidays, cancelled classes and extra classes.
  • Syncs across your phone and laptop.

Who it's for

College students aged 18 and over who need to keep their attendance above a minimum. BunkKro is free.

How to start

Open BunkKro and create an account with your email, or continue with Google.

Good to know

BunkKro's numbers are estimates from what you enter. Your college's records are the official ones, so check them before relying on a bunk. BunkKro shows no ads and doesn't sell your data; see the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
