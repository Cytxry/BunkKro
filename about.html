Can I bunk today?

BunkKro is a free attendance tracker for college students. Mark your classes as they happen, and it works out how many you can still skip without dropping below your college's minimum.

Open BunkKro

What it does

  • Tracks every subject. Your attendance percentage for each subject and overall, rounded the way your college rounds it.
  • Answers “can I bunk?”. How many classes you can miss, or how many you need to attend to get back to your target.
  • Knows your week. Upload a photo or PDF of your timetable and BunkKro reads it, or set it up by hand. Day Order colleges work too.
  • Handles odd days. Holidays, cancelled classes, extra classes and “Saturday follows Monday”.
  • Works everywhere. Your data syncs across your phone and laptop, and a browser extension lets you mark classes in a click.

Good to know

BunkKro's numbers are estimates worked out from what you enter. Your college's records are the ones that count, so check them before you rely on a bunk, especially near exams.

BunkKro is free. A paid Pro plan is planned; it isn't available yet.

Your data

BunkKro stores your account and the attendance you enter so it can sync across your devices. It shows no ads and doesn't sell your data. You can download or delete everything from Settings at any time. The privacy policy has the details.
