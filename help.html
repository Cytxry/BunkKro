1. How does BunkKro work out how many classes I can miss?

It uses the classes you've attended and missed, your target percentage, and, if you've set them, your semester dates and timetable to know how many classes are left. It then tells you how many you can miss and still finish at or above your target, or how many you need to attend to get back there. The numbers are estimates from what you enter; your college's records are what count.

2. My college's percentage is different from BunkKro's

Colleges round differently. In Settings → Attendance, set How your college rounds to Down, Up or Exact, and check your target. Also check that each subject's attended and missed counts match your college's portal: open the subject and edit its numbers.

3. How do I add my timetable?

Open Smart Timetable and upload a photo or PDF of it. BunkKro AI reads it (the first time, BunkKro asks whether to send it to BunkKro AI or use the basic scanner in your browser; see the Privacy Policy) and shows you what it found before anything is saved. A scan or photo takes a minute or two. You can also build or edit the timetable by hand on the same page.

4. The timetable came out wrong

Before you confirm, tap any class to fix its subject or time, and remove anything that isn't a class. A clear, straight, well-lit photo or the original PDF gives the best result. After saving, you can still edit the timetable on the Smart Timetable page.

5. Holidays, cancelled and extra classes

On Daily Classes, mark a class as Cancelled so it doesn't count against you, mark a whole day as a holiday, add an extra class, or set a day that follows another day's timetable. Upcoming holidays can be added ahead of time.

6. I forgot my password

On the login screen, type your email and tap Forgot password?. You'll get an email with a link to set a new one. If you signed up with Google, use Continue with Google instead; there's no separate password. To change a password you know, go to Settings → Account.

7. How do I download or delete my data?

In Settings → Your data, Download my data gives you everything in one file. Delete my data removes it from every device, and then lets you delete your account too. Deleting can't be undone. The Privacy Policy explains what we store and why.
